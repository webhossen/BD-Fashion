Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers

What file formats do you accept for uploads? ▼

We accept PNG, JPG, GIF, and SVG files. The maximum file size is 10MB. For best results, use high-resolution images (300 DPI or higher).

How long does shipping take? ▼

Dhaka: 1-2 business days | Outside Dhaka: 3-5 business days | International: 7-14 business days. Orders are processed within 24 hours and then shipped.

Can I edit my order after placing it? ▼

Yes! You can edit your order within 24 hours of placing it. After that, the order goes to production. Contact our support team for any changes.

What payment methods do you accept? ▼

We accept Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Debit Cards, PayPal, and bKash mobile payments. All payments are secure and encrypted.

Do you offer bulk discounts? ▼

Yes! Orders of 10+ units get 20% off, 25+ units get 30% off, and 50+ units get 40% off. Contact our sales team for custom bulk orders.

What's your return/refund policy? ▼

We offer 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with your order, we'll remake it or provide a full refund within 30 days.

Can I save my designs? ▼

Absolutely! Create an account to save all your designs. You can access, edit, and reorder them anytime from your profile.

Do you use environmentally friendly methods? ▼

Yes! We use DTG (Direct-to-Garment) printing which is eco-friendly and produces minimal waste. We're committed to sustainable practices.
